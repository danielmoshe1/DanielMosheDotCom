About

Daniel Moshe is a portfolio entrepreneur and Expert EOS Implementer based in Wayzata, Minnesota, where he lives with his two sons. He founded his first company, Tech Guru, in 2004 while studying at the University of Minnesota's Carlson School of Management — and quickly discovered the real challenges of entrepreneurship firsthand. That experience led him to EOS (the Entrepreneurial Operating System), which he encountered through an entrepreneurial accelerator program and has used to run his own businesses ever since.

Over the past decade-plus, Daniel has guided dozens of leadership teams across industries as an Expert EOS Implementer — helping them clarify their vision, build accountability, and gain Traction®. He speaks nationwide and brings the credibility of an active, practicing entrepreneur to every engagement.

He is the founder of Lucid Day (2019), a monday.com consulting firm, and remains actively involved with Tech Guru, the technology services company he has led for over 20 years. He also volunteers as a coach with the Entrepreneurs' Organization Accelerator Program, supporting early-stage founders building strong foundations for growth.

Outside of business, Daniel is a licensed pilot and an avid motorcyclist.
