The Story

From a dorm room at the U of M
to a portfolio of companies.

Daniel Moshe is a portfolio entrepreneur, national speaker, and Expert EOS Implementer™ based in the Minneapolis area, where he lives with his two sons, Isaac and Jonah, and the family dog, Eddie. He founded his first company, Tech Guru, in 2004 while studying entrepreneurship and management information systems at the University of Minnesota's Carlson School of Management — and discovered the real challenges of building a business firsthand.

That experience led him to EOS® — the Entrepreneurial Operating System® — which he encountered through an entrepreneurial accelerator program. He has run every one of his businesses on EOS since 2011, and in 2016 he did what most founders only dream about: he worked himself out of a day-to-day job in his own company.

Since then, Daniel has delivered more than 500 sessions for leadership teams across industries as an Expert EOS Implementer™ — helping them crystallize their vision, instill discipline and accountability, and gain real Traction® — all while continuing to start and grow technology companies. Known as "the caring entrepreneur," he serves as a mentor in the Entrepreneurs' Organization (EO) Accelerator program, guiding early-stage founders building strong foundations for growth, and is passionate about making a positive impact in the world.
